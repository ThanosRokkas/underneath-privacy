# Delete Your Account

**Effective Date:** July 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account for **Underneath**, a food label scanning application operated by Thanos Rokkas, and what data is deleted or kept when you do.

**Contact:** [thanosrokkas@gmail.com](mailto:thanosrokkas@gmail.com)
**Website:** [underneath.app](https://underneath.app)

---

## How to Delete Your Account

### Option 1: In the App

1. Open the Underneath app.
2. Go to your **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

### Option 2: By Email

Send a request to [thanosrokkas@gmail.com](mailto:thanosrokkas@gmail.com) from the email address associated with your account. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

---

## What Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is **permanently removed** through a cascading delete:

- Your account and profile
- Health and diet preferences synced to our servers
- Subscription records
- Referral records
- Scan history tied to your user ID

Local data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

---

## What Is Kept

- **Anonymized cached product data** (barcodes, product names, and health scores) that is not linked to you. This non-personal data is retained to keep the service fast for all users.
- **Minimal records required to meet legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations**, where applicable. No additional retention period applies beyond that.
